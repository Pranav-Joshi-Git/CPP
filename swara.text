================================================================
                         THE CORE IDEA
================================================================

        The text appears to be saying:

        What happens in the COSMOS
                    ↕
        what happens in the BODY
                    ↕
        what happens in PRANA
                    ↕
        what happens in the NADIS
                    ↕
        what appears as SVARA
                    ↕
        what can be OBSERVED through BREATH
                    ↕
        can reveal something about the STATE OF REALITY.

        Therefore:

              BREATH
                ↓
             SVARA
                ↓
             PRANA
                ↓
             NADIS
                ↓
            TATTVAS
                ↓
          BODY ↔ COSMOS
                ↓
          SELF / ATMAN


================================================================
                    ONE IMPORTANT CAVEAT
================================================================

This map combines the conceptual relationships described across
the first 100 verses.

It is NOT saying that:

    "The text scientifically proves that all these things
     physically exist in exactly this arrangement."

Nor are all arrows equally explicit in the verses.

Rather, this is the WORLDVIEW / MODEL that seems to emerge
when we put the verses together.

That distinction will become important as we go deeper.
