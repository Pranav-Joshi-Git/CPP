A few things I would deliberately keep "open" in this map

These are not errors to fix right now. They are precisely the things we should eventually explore.

[TENSION 1]
Sushumna:
worldly adverse ↔ spiritually essential

[TENSION 2]
Tattva → outcome:
the same tattva can receive different outcomes
depending on context.

[TENSION 3]
Lunar / Solar:
neither is simply "good" or "bad"; their usefulness
depends heavily on the intended action.

[TENSION 4]
Conception:
multiple Svara/tattva rules produce different outcomes.
We should map their conditions before attempting reconciliation.

[TENSION 5]
Death prediction:
many apparently precise prognostic rules exist.
We should distinguish:
    observation
    symbolic interpretation
    textual prediction
    actual empirical validity.

[TENSION 6]
Cosmic ↔ bodily correspondence:
the text repeatedly treats them as connected, but the
exact mechanism of that correspondence is something to
understand rather than assume.

[TENSION 7]
"Control" of another person:
the enchantment material belongs to the text's traditional
framework, but its literal mechanism should not be assumed.
